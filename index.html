Wearable Health Device Pricing Model

Machine Learning

Built and compared three machine learning models in Python (scikit-learn) to predict whether a wearable health device — Fitbit, Apple Watch, WHOOP, and similar — sits in the premium or non-premium tier, based on accuracy, sensors, battery life, and engineered pricing features. Logistic Regression identified which features drive premium pricing, Random Forest reached ~99.8% test accuracy after tuning with GridSearchCV, and K-Means clustering uncovered natural low/mid/high-end market segments.

PJM Electricity Load Forecasting

Energy Markets

Forecast hourly electricity demand for PJM — the regional transmission organization covering 13 U.S. states and D.C. — on ~145,000 hours of public load data (2002–2018). Engineered lag and rolling-average features on top of calendar structure, then trained a Gradient Boosting Regressor with a proper time-based train/test split (no leakage). The model cut forecast error by 57% versus a naive "same hour last week" baseline, landing at 4.86% MAPE on a held-out year of data — the kind of short-term demand forecast that grid operators and energy traders rely on to schedule generation and price electricity.
